01

The pivot

Asked for

An AI assistant that recommends investments

I proposed

Answers about your own financial documents, running fully on your computer

  • Cent Capital's idea meant handling people's private financial data under heavy regulation.
  • I proposed something narrower: plain-English questions about statements, invoices, pay stubs and 1099s. The data never leaves your computer. It is built to quote its source for each answer, and to refuse when it can't find support.
  • I wrote the spec and the scope (no bank linking, no advice, synthetic data only), worked weekly with the co-founder, and built it with AI coding tools.
  • Cent Capital has since open-sourced it, crediting me as the original author.

02

How I decided what "good" means

  • An 80-question test set with the expected source for each answer.
  • An AI judge, checked against 20 of my own labels (a small check, so weak evidence on its own), plus a regression check that fails the build if search quality drops.
  • Every result tied to the exact code, settings and test set that produced it.
  • Hybrid search plus a reranker raised MRR (how high the right source ranks) from 0.50 to 0.79.

03

Decisions: what I kept and what I rejected

Decision

Set the bar before the test, and don't ship a change that fails it.

  • KeptThe 8B model already in use. Six local models, including the 8B, compared on 480 scored answers; none beat the 8B.
  • Not adoptedThe five alternative models.
  • RejectedA new prompt. It passed 5 of 6 preset checks but turned 3 refusals into wrong answers.
  • RejectedA citation checker. It retracted 28 answers that were already correct.
  • A prototype agent with calculator and SQL tools did better on cross-document totals with the 8B model (17% to 58%, on just 12 questions) but worse with the smaller 4B model. It stayed a prototype; the shipped product uses hybrid search.

04

Safety and limits

  • Guardrails: personal details masked and restored exactly, number checks against the source, and isolation between test personas.
  • Blocked 6 of 6 planted data leaks.
  • Limits: the test set and documents are synthetic and self-written, there are no real users yet, and over-refusal was not meaningfully tested. These are measured, reproducible numbers, not a claim that it is production-ready.

New-seller activation rose from 17% to 28% after I reshaped the same ₹1,200 incentive

Meesho · Associate Product Manager, Supplier Acquisition and Activation · Jun 2022 to Jun 2023

Meesho onboarded 1,500 new sellers a day, many selling online for the first time. The team's North Star: new sellers with 5 orders delivered in their first 45 days.

I owned version 2 of the new-seller incentive.

What I owned

  • Incentive V2, the 3-step ladder
  • Seller calls and the banner tests
  • The credit qualification job
  • Abuse rules with Trust and Safety

With the team

Design drew the banner, finance automated the payout, Trust and Safety built the detection, and a big team ran the nudges and calls.

Combined result of the V2 program, not one change.

Version 1 showed no lift because sellers missed it, took it for an ad, or couldn't qualify

Version 1: list 7 products within 15 days and get ₹1,200 in ad credits. My A/B test showed no significant lift.

So I called 15 to 20 sellers, ran a second round of calls when the data couldn't explain the banner, and checked the listing data.

I split the same ₹1,200 into a 3-step ladder, so the first reward came sooner

The call

Same total reward, better shape.

₹300 at 3 products, ₹400 more at 5, ₹500 more at 7. The first reward comes at 3, because 7 was too high a bar and sellers were re-uploading products to reach it.

Four changes shipped together, and no single one gets the credit for 17% to 28%

Abuse rules with Trust and Safety ran alongside: repeat accounts stopped receiving credits, but could still sell.

Combined result of the V2 program. The click-through figures come from A/B tests.

More from Meesho

Image-first catalog upload

Sellers listed products by filling a long Excel sheet. With data science, we let sellers upload a photo first; an image-recognition model prefilled the attributes. Upload drop-off fell 7 points and upload time fell by 2 to 3 minutes per seller in the first months.

GPT product descriptions (2023)

Data showed products with poor descriptions sold less than similar products. I ran the research and worked with engineering to ship GPT-written descriptions from image labels and product attributes. Launched shortly before I left, so I don't have a result to report.

Numbers are rounded.

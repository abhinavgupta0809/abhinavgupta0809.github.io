Meesho: new-seller activation from 17% to 28%

The bet
The ₹1,200 ad-credit incentive for new sellers showed no significant lift. A better-shaped version, at the same reward per seller, could get more of them to their first orders.
What I did
Called sellers, then shipped version 2: a 3-step ladder, a new banner, a progress bar and credits in about 6 hours.
What happened
Activation (5 orders in 45 days) rose from 17% to 28%, the combined result of version 2.

01

The goal

  • Meesho onboards 1,500+ new sellers a day (about 600K in my year there), many of them selling online for the first time.
  • The team's North Star: the share of new sellers with 5 orders delivered in their first 45 days. It sat at 17%.
  • The lever was an incentive: free ad credits for new sellers. I was given ownership of version 2 of it.

02

Why version 1 wasn't working

  • V1: list 7 products within 15 days and get ₹1,200 in ad credits. My A/B test showed no significant lift.
  • I called 15 to 20 sellers. Many didn't know the incentive existed.
  • A second round of calls found why: the banner featured a TV actor known from ads, so sellers took it for an advertisement, and its colors clashed with the page.
  • The data showed new sellers re-uploading the same product to reach 7. Duplicates were auto-deleted, so they never qualified.

03

What I changed

Decision

A 3-step ladder with the same reward per seller

₹300 at 3 products, ₹400 more at 5, ₹500 more at 7. Same ₹1,200. The first reward comes at 3 products, because 7 was too high a bar.

  • Banner redesign with the design team, A/B tested: click-through went from 18% to 35% to about 45%.
  • Clearer progress: a progress bar replaced a circular graphic, plus a notification at each step.
  • Faster credits: I replaced a weekly manual list with a job that ran every 3 to 4 hours; finance automated the payout. Delivery went from 4 to 5 days to about 6 hours.
  • Abuse rules with Trust and Safety: repeat accounts were flagged and stopped receiving credits, while they could still sell.

04

Results

17% to 28%
Activation (5 orders in 45 days)
  • Activation rose as the combined result of the V2 program, not one change.

More from Meesho

Image-first catalog upload

Sellers listed products by filling a long Excel sheet. With data science, we let sellers upload a photo first; an image-recognition model prefilled the attributes. Upload drop-off fell 7 points and upload time fell by 2 to 3 minutes per seller in the first months.

GPT product descriptions (2023)

Data showed products with poor descriptions sold less than similar products. I ran the research and worked with engineering to ship GPT-written descriptions from image labels and product attributes. Launched shortly before I left, so I don't have a result to report.

  2. Teardown

    Run Receipt: what did that run cost me?

    Live

    A teardown of credit predictability in Perplexity. One receipt, shown at three moments of a run, starting with a cost range before you send.

    The signal: 202 reviews from paying users who ran out of credits.

    Limits: these are my own runs, not user interviews. Independent and unofficial. Not affiliated with Perplexity.

    Open Run ReceiptDashboardRepo

  3. Research

    App review dashboards

    Live

    For each target company I scrape 90 days of Google Play and App Store reviews, label every one with an LLM, check the labels against a hand-labeled set, and publish what users praise, where they hurt and what they ask for.

    • Perplexity14,791 reviews labeledDashboardRepo
    • Claude app26,000 reviews labeledDashboardRepo
    • Indeed46,454 reviews labeledRepoRepo only

    Store reviews are self-selected, so I treat these as signals, not validated problems. Independent and unofficial. Not affiliated with Perplexity, Anthropic or Indeed.

How I work

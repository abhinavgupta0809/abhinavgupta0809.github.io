Flipkart Black: ~500K paid members in four months

The bet
Flipkart's earlier premium tier wasn't connecting with members. Relaunch it around a name people already knew, with a video benefit to match rival memberships.
What I did
I ran the user research behind the positioning, defined the target segments, designed the launch notifications, chose who saw the checkout upsell, and built the data case shown to partners.
What happened
Paid members grew 2.5x from month one to month four. YouTube Premium joined as a Black benefit; Netflix followed.

01

What it is and what I owned

  • Flipkart Black is Flipkart's premium paid membership. It replaced an earlier premium tier, so this was a relaunch, not a brand-new product.
  • I was a product manager on a large loyalty team.

What I owned

  • User research
  • Positioning
  • Segmentation and targeting
  • Launch tracking
  • The data case for partner benefits

Not mine

Pricing and eligibility were decided above me. I gave input on the launch price and built the segmentations the eligibility decision used.

02

The problem and the research

  • The earlier tier wasn't connecting with members, and it had no video benefit to set against rival memberships.
  • I ran a user study on naming. "Black" resonated because people already knew it from Uber Black and Swiggy Black, so the membership was rebuilt around it.
  • SuperCoins rules were hard to follow: earn caps, spend caps, and only a small share of products eligible. Black members could spend coins on any product, with higher caps.

Decision

I positioned the relaunch around the name the study picked: a word members already knew.

03

Going to market

  • I defined the target segments and designed the landing page and the push, email and WhatsApp notifications.
  • Checkout upsell: shoppers saw what Black would save them on the order in front of them. My analysis chose who saw it.
  • The data pipeline wasn't ready at launch, so I built the tracking dashboards in an internal tool as a workaround.

Decision

Don't launch blind. With no pipeline, build the tracking in the tool that existed so the launch could be read from day one.

04

The video benefit and the results

  • I built the data case shown to Google and Netflix. YouTube Premium launched as a Black benefit; Netflix followed later as a second benefit.

More from Flipkart

Treats, gamified rewards for members

I owned what to build, the design, instrumentation and the monitoring dashboard. Members earned rewards after a set number of purchases. Purchase frequency rose 8% and retention 3% for the same members after launch (before and after, not an A/B test).

Planning model behind a $50M loyalty budget

Started as a simulator for one membership program: how many people would join, which benefits they'd use, and what it would cost, built on 2 to 3 years of usage data. Business leaders then asked for it across Flipkart loyalty. I built the model; the business team made the planning calls.

Executive reporting

A 100-metric loyalty dashboard reviewed by Flipkart's CEO, and weekly analysis presented to the VP of Loyalty.

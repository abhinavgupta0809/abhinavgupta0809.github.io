Flipkart Black reached 500K paid members four months after a relaunch I researched, positioned and targeted

Flipkart (Walmart) · Product Manager, Loyalty · Dec 2024 to Jan 2026

Flipkart is India's largest e-commerce website, with 400 million customers, and is owned by Walmart. Flipkart Black is a membership designed in response to Amazon Prime. I was one PM on a large loyalty team.

More detail

Pricing and eligibility sat above me. I gave input on the launch price and built the segmentations behind the eligibility call.

Members didn't connect with the old tier, so I positioned the relaunch around a name they knew

My naming study: people already knew “Black” from Uber Black and Swiggy Black, so the relaunch took that name.

More detail

The earlier tier wasn't landing, and it had no video benefit to set against rival memberships. I ran the user study on naming and positioned the relaunch around the name it picked.

My segmentation chose who saw the checkout upsell, and I read the launch from day one

The upsell showed shoppers what Black would save on the order in front of them. My analysis chose who saw it.

The call

Show the checkout upsell to the shoppers my analysis picked.

More detail

I defined the target segments and designed the landing page and the push, email and WhatsApp notifications. No data pipeline was ready at launch, so I built the tracking in the tool that existed and read the launch from day one.

I built the partner data case; YouTube Premium launched on Black and hit 350K activations in three months

I built the data case shown to Google and Netflix. YouTube Premium launched first; Netflix followed as a second benefit.

350K is YouTube Premium only. The data case was mine; the partnership was a team result.

More from Flipkart

Treats, gamified rewards for members

I owned what to build, the design, instrumentation and the monitoring dashboard. Members earned rewards after a set number of purchases. Purchase frequency rose 8% and retention 3% for the same members after launch (before and after, not an A/B test).

Planning model behind a $50M loyalty budget

Started as a simulator for one membership program: how many people would join, which benefits they'd use, and what it would cost, built on 2 to 3 years of usage data. Business leaders then asked for it across Flipkart loyalty. I built the model; the business team made the planning calls.

Executive reporting

A 100-metric loyalty dashboard reviewed by Flipkart's CEO, and weekly analysis presented to the VP of Loyalty.

Tata 1mg: $145K a year saved, GenAI video in 15+ languages

The bet
WhatsApp could replace agent calls for invalid prescriptions, and short GenAI videos could explain a medicine better than a long page most users only skimmed.
What I did
Designed and instrumented the WhatsApp flow, built the first GenAI medicine videos myself, and worked on test booking and how prices were shown.
What happened
80% of cases resolved with no call, about $145K a year saved, and medicine videos in 15+ Indian languages.

02

WhatsApp prescription recovery

  • Prescription medicines need a valid prescription at checkout. Many uploads weren't valid, and a call-center agent had to call each user.
  • The new flow: on an invalid upload, the user got up to three WhatsApp messages over about three hours. Each had a personal link to remove the medicine, upload a new prescription, or book an online consult with a Tata 1mg doctor whose prescription attached automatically.
  • My part: designed the messages; worked with engineering on the personal links and tested them with 100 employees; wrote the instrumentation and built the dashboard.
  • Not mine: the pilot on 10% of users and the decision to scale were made by my manager and team.

03

GenAI medicine videos

  • Our user study found reading a full medicine page took 6 to 7 minutes, users spent a couple, and English wasn't most users' first language.
  • I built the first version myself for 5 to 10 medicines: GPT wrote short scripts from the medicine information, Synthesia made 30-second videos, in 15+ Indian languages.
  • The content team checked every script for medical accuracy and reviewed every video before it went live. Engineering scaled it to about 50 medicines.
  • Conversion on the ~50 medicine pages with videos rose 3%.

04

At-home test booking

  • At-home lab tests grew from about 2,000 to about 50,000 orders a day. Many teams worked on it; my part was the booking flow and how prices were shown.
  • Coupon page, which I owned: ranked the offers that apply to your cart by savings, best first. Redemptions rose 12%.

Decision

I pushed for a test on 20% of traffic instead of a full launch, expecting a surprise price at checkout to raise drop-off. It did, and the idea was dropped.

  • RejectedHide time-slot prices until checkout.
  • Booking conversion rose 7 points over 8 to 10 months, a team result across booking design, coupons and test bundles.

Numbers are rounded.

I pushed for a 20% test on hidden slot prices, and the result killed the idea

The call

Test it on 20% of traffic instead of launching it.

A proposal would hide time-slot prices until checkout. I expected a surprise price to raise drop-off. It did, and the idea was dropped.

No drop-off size on record. The direction, not the number, settled it.
